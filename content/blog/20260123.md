## Research

- Staking decentralization & ops resilience:
  
  - <a target="_new" rel="noopener" class="decorated-link" href="https://ethresear.ch/t/native-dvt-for-ethereum-staking/23894?utm_source=chatgpt.com">Native DVT for Ethereum staking</a> proposed protocol-enshrined DVT: allowing a single validator (with enough balance) to register multiple keys with an m-of-n threshold, aiming to reduce operational complexity vs. off-protocol DVT while improving security/decentralization properties.
- Execution-layer scalability & worst-case behavior:
  
  - <a target="_new" rel="noopener" class="decorated-link" href="https://ethresear.ch/t/worst-case-block-size-and-calldata-repricing-for-glamsterdam/23895?utm_source=chatgpt.com">Worst-Case Block Size and Calldata Repricing for Glamsterdam</a> analyzed a new worst-case block-size construction and discussed repricing directions (incl. access-list and calldata-floor repricing ideas) in the context of higher gas limits / blob counts and future PBS timelines.
- Applications / UX bridging physical ↔ onchain:
  
  - <a target="_new" rel="noopener" class="decorated-link" href="https://ethresear.ch/t/crypto-native-cash-bridging-physical-cash-and-defi/23876?utm_source=chatgpt.com">Crypto Native Cash: Bridging physical Cash and DeFi</a> proposed using ERC-4337-style account abstraction + NFC-bound “physical possession” keys and a multi-key governance model to approximate cash-like transfer UX while retaining onchain controls (e.g., freezing/recovery).
