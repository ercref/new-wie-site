## Research

- <a target="_new" rel="noopener" class="decorated-link" href="https://ethresear.ch/t/demystifying-blockchain-kv-lookups-from-o-log-n-to-o-1-disk-i-o/23711?utm_source=chatgpt.com">“Demystifying Blockchain KV Lookups: From O(log N) to O(1) Disk I/O”</a>: reports controlled Pebble experiments (22GB → 2.2TB) arguing that once Bloom filters + top index blocks are cached, random lookup I/O collapses toward ~1–2 disk reads (and can further approach ~1–1.3 when index blocks fit), materially changing how we should model EL database bottlenecks.
- <a target="_new" rel="noopener" class="decorated-link" href="https://ethresear.ch/t/wormholes-and-the-cost-of-plausible-deniability/23728?utm_source=chatgpt.com">“Wormholes and the cost of plausible deniability”</a>: argues wormholes’ “plausible deniability” property appears in tension with Ethereum’s 160-bit address space, and that EIP-7503’s implied anonymity set is lower than expected in practice; ends with a sketched alternative direction using beacon-chain deposits.
- <a target="_new" rel="noopener" class="decorated-link" href="https://ethresear.ch/t/does-fhe-deserve-this-much-attention/23726?utm_source=chatgpt.com">“Does FHE deserve this much attention?”</a>: surveys why FHE is resurging (general computation over encrypted state), but highlights that many “FHEVM-style” designs still rely on MPC committees for key management/decryption; points to newer directions aiming to reduce committee trust (e.g., the linked “Root iO” paper).
